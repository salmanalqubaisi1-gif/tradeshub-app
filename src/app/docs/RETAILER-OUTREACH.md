## Status Meanings

- Waiting = outreach sent, awaiting response
- Follow Up = no response after reasonable wait
- Interested = retailer replied positively
- Approved = retailer authorized product information sharing
- Rejected = retailer declined
- Blocked = no usable contact route yet
